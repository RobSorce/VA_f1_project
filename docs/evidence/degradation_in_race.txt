=== C. Lap noise: pooled residual sigma of the whole-race stint fits, per race ===
Prior of a kind of race (computePriors, lapNoisePrior): the per-race variances as a scaled-inverse-chi-square, moments
matched: nu0 = 4 + 2 / cv^2 (cv: coefficient of variation of the variances), s0 = sqrt(mean x (nu0 - 2) / nu0).
lapNoiseFromFits uses s0 only before the race's first fit; from the first fit a race at its clock uses its own pooled
sigma, sqrt(sse / dof) over its fits so far (no prior mixed in).
